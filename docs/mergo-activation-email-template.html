How to use this (this yellow box is not part of the email):
  1. In Gmail (the account that runs Mergo), press Compose. Subject: Activate your JPSME account. Leave To empty.
  2. Click the blue Copy email for Gmail button below.
  3. Click inside the empty Gmail draft body and press Ctrl+V. You should see the yellow button — not code.
  4. Check the links: click the button text in the draft and press Ctrl+K. The web address must be exactly {{Activation Link}}. Fix it if it is anything else. Do the same for the long link line.
  5. Close the draft (it saves itself). In Mergo, pick this draft and the Email column, then send a test to yourself first.

Hi {{First Name}},

A JPSME administrator has created an account for you, using this email address.

Open the link below to finish setting it up. You will choose your own password and pick your school or organization.

Activate my account

Or paste this into your browser:
{{Activation Link}}

This link works for 3 days and can be used once.
Until you do, the account cannot be signed in to. Nobody — including the administrator who created it — knows or can see your password.
If you were not expecting this, you can ignore this email and nothing will happen.
