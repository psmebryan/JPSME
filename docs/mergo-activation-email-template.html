How the Mergo activation email is set up
  1. JPSME fills the campaign sheet. Each row has an Activation Link (the plain link) and an Activation Button (a Sheets formula, =HYPERLINK(link, IMAGE(button picture)), that Mergo sends as a clickable yellow button).
  2. In Gmail (the account that runs Mergo), press Compose. Subject: Activate your JPSME account. Leave To empty.
  3. Paste the text in the white box below as ordinary text. Do not use Gmail's link button (Ctrl+K) at all: Gmail will not accept a Mergo marker as a web address, and does not need to — the markers are replaced when Mergo sends.
  4. Close the draft. In Mergo, pick this draft and the Email column, and send a test to yourself first.
Rows added before the button column existed have no button; their email still carries the plain link.
