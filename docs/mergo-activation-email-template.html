How to use this (the yellow box is not part of the email):
  1. In Gmail, press Compose. Subject: Activate your JPSME account
  2. Click inside the white box below, press Ctrl+A once it is the only thing selected (or drag-select from "Hi" to the last line), then Ctrl+C.
  3. Paste into the Gmail draft body with Ctrl+V.
  4. Check both links: in the draft, click the yellow Activate my account button text and press Ctrl+K. The web address must be exactly {{Activation Link}} — copying from a browser can turn it into a file:/// address, so fix it if it is anything else. Do the same for the long link line.
  5. Leave the draft in Drafts. In Mergo, pick this draft and the Email column, then send a test to yourself first.

Hi {{First Name}},

A JPSME administrator has created an account for you, using this email address.

Open the link below to finish setting it up. You will choose your own password and pick your school or organization.

Activate my account

Or paste this into your browser:
{{Activation Link}}

This link works for 3 days and can be used once.
Until you do, the account cannot be signed in to. Nobody — including the administrator who created it — knows or can see your password.
If you were not expecting this, you can ignore this email and nothing will happen.
